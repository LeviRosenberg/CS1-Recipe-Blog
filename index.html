Chicken Teriyaki

Description

Chicken teriyaki is a popular Japanese dish made with chicken covered in a sweet and savory sauce. It is usually served with rice.

Ingredients

  • 2 chicken breasts
  • 2 cups cooked rice
  • 3 tablespoons soy sauce
  • 2 tablespoons honey
  • 1 tablespoon mirin
  • 1 teaspoon ginger
  • 1 teaspoon cooking oil

Steps

  1. Mix the soy sauce, honey, mirin, and ginger in a bowl.
  2. Cut the chicken into small pieces.
  3. Heat the oil in a pan and cook the chicken until fully cooked.
  4. Add the sauce and simmer until it thickens.
  5. Serve the chicken over rice.

Chicken Katsu Curry

Description

Chicken katsu curry is a Japanese dish made with crispy breaded chicken, flavorful curry sauce, and rice.

Ingredients

  • 2 chicken breasts
  • 1/2 cup flour
  • 1 egg
  • 1 cup panko breadcrumbs
  • 1 onion
  • 1 carrot
  • 2 cups chicken broth
  • 2 Japanese curry roux cubes
  • 2 cups cooked rice
  • Cooking oil

Steps

  1. Flatten the chicken breasts to an even thickness.
  2. Coat the chicken in flour, egg, and panko breadcrumbs.
  3. Cook the chicken in oil until golden and fully cooked.
  4. Chop the onion and carrot and cook them in a pot.
  5. Add the broth and simmer until the carrot is tender.
  6. Stir in the curry roux until the sauce thickens.
  7. Serve the chicken and curry over rice.

Miso Ramen

Description

Miso ramen is a Japanese noodle soup made with a savory broth containing miso paste. It can be topped with eggs, mushrooms, and scallions.

Ingredients

  • 2 portions ramen noodles
  • 4 cups chicken or vegetable broth
  • 2 tablespoons miso paste
  • 1 tablespoon soy sauce
  • 1 teaspoon sesame oil
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 1 teaspoon ginger
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 cup sliced mushrooms

Steps

  1. Boil the eggs for 7–9 minutes, then peel and cut them in half.
  2. Heat sesame oil in a pot and cook the garlic, ginger, and mushrooms.
  3. Add the broth and soy sauce and simmer for 10 minutes.
  4. Lower the heat and stir in the miso paste.
  5. Cook the ramen noodles according to the package directions.
  6. Place the noodles in bowls and pour the broth over them.
  7. Add the eggs and serve.
